Bring your Pokémon in

The game doesn't let other apps read your account, so your collection comes from a Poke Genie export. It takes a minute.
  1. In Poke Genie, open your scan history and export it as CSV (all your scanned Pokémon).
  2. Save the file to your phone or computer.
  3. Upload it here. Upload a newer export whenever you want to refresh.
Your collection is stored only in your account. It powers "your counters", "your team" and "only what I own" everywhere in the app.
